Privacy Policy

App Name: Taskprayuj

Developer: Amit Kumar

Last Updated: February 13, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how personal data is collected, used, stored, shared, and protected when the Taskprayuj Android app is used. Compliance is maintained with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and Google Play Store Developer Policies.

2. Permissions and Device Access

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used for task reminders, habit streaks, and Pomodoro alerts.
  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Used exclusively for the "Voice Journaling" feature. Audio is processed via system APIs to convert speech to text and is not stored as audio files on our servers.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID): Used by Google/Firebase services for app analytics and unique user counting. You can reset this in your Android device settings.

3. Data Collection and Usage

  • Authentication: Name, Email, and hashed passwords are processed via Firebase Authentication.
  • Local Storage: Tasks, habits, and journal entries are stored locally in a secure Room Database on your device.
  • Analytics & Diagnostics: Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics are used to understand app usage and identify bugs.
  • AI Integration: Anonymized snippets from journals may be processed by Gemini AI models to provide habit suggestions. No personally identifiable information (PII) is permanently associated with these AI queries.

4. User Control: Analytics and Crash Reporting

  • Opt-Out Toggle: Disable all non-essential data collection at any time via Settings > Data & Analytics.
  • Default State: Tracking is limited until you provide consent and can be revoked instantly.

5. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell your data. Data is shared only with:

  • Google Firebase: Authentication, analytics, crash reporting, and cloud messaging.
  • Google Gemini: AI-powered productivity insights (Anonymized).
  • Legal Necessity: If required by law or to protect user safety.

6. Storage and Retention

  • User Content: Remains on your device until uninstalled or cleared.
  • Analytics Data: Retained ~60 days in de-identified format.
  • Account Data: Retained until you request deletion.

7. User Rights (DPDP Act & GDPR)

  • Access & Correction: View and update your data within the app.
  • Deletion: Delete your account and associated data via the Delete Account button.
  • Withdraw Consent: Revoke permissions or disable analytics via settings.

8. Data Security

All data transmitted between the app and Firebase servers is encrypted using HTTPS (TLS). Industry-standard security measures are implemented to prevent unauthorized access or breaches.

9. Children's Privacy

Taskprayuj is intended for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from individuals under 18.
