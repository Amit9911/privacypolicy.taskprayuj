Taskprayuj Privacy Policy

App Name: Taskprayuj

Developer: Amit Kumar

Last Updated: March 18, 2026

1. Overview➕

This Privacy Policy outlines how personal data is collected, used, stored, shared, and protected when you use the Taskprayuj Android application. We are committed to maintaining your privacy and ensuring compliance with relevant data protection laws, including India’s DPDP Act, GDPR, and Google Play Store Developer Policies. This updated policy also addresses the integration of advertising services through Google AdMob.

2. Permissions and Device Access➕
  • Notifications: Task reminders and other updates (Taskprayuj uses Firebase Cloud Messaging and Firebase In‑App Messaging to provide contextual guidance, contextual nudges and feature updates) .
  • Microphone: Used only for Voice Journaling, processed locally.
  • Advertising ID: Used for analytics and personalized ads; can be reset anytime.
3. Data Collection and Usage➕
  • Authentication: Name, Email, hashed passwords via Firebase.
  • Local Storage: Tasks, habits, journals stored securely on device.
  • Analytics & Diagnostics: Firebase Analytics , Google Analytics and Crashlytics.
  • AI Integration: Anonymized journal snippets processed by Google Gemini AI.
  • Advertising Data: AdMob collects identifiers and ad interaction data.
4. User Control and Consent➕
  • Opt-out of analytics and crash reporting via Settings > Data & Analytics.
  • Manage personalized advertising via device settings or Google Ad Settings.
  • Request deletion of personal data as outlined in Section 7.
5. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services➕

We do not sell your personal data. Data is shared only with following trusted third-party providers:

  • Google Firebase: Authentication, analytics, crash reporting, and messaging.
  • Google Gemini: Anonymized data processed for productivity insights.
  • Google AdMob: Integrated for advertisements, governed by Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Google Analytics: Used for tracking app usage and performance.
  • Legal Necessity: Disclosure if required by law or to protect rights and safety.
6. Storage and Retention➕
  • User Content: Stored locally until app uninstall or data clear.
  • Analytics Data: Retained ~60 days in de-identified format.
  • Account Data: Retained until account deletion request.
7. User Rights (DPDP Act & GDPR)➕
  • Access & Correction: View/update personal data in app settings.
  • Deletion: Delete account and data via the Delete Account button.
  • Withdraw Consent: Revoke permissions or disable non-essential data collection anytime.
  • Grievance Redressal: Contact developer for concerns; resolved within 30 days.
8. Data Security➕

All data transmitted between Taskprayuj and Firebase servers is encrypted using HTTPS (TLS). We maintain safeguards to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, or destruction of your information.

9. Google AdMob Integration➕

Taskprayuj integrates Google AdMob to display advertisements within the app. This integration allows us to offer the app for free while generating revenue to support ongoing development and maintenance. The following outlines how data is handled in relation to AdMob:

  • Data Collected by AdMob: AdMob may collect information such as your device’s advertising ID, IP address, and interaction data with ads (e.g., clicks, impressions). This data is used to serve personalized ads and measure ad performance.
  • User Control: You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads through your device settings or by visiting Google’s Ad Settings page.
  • Privacy Policy: For more details on how Google handles data in relation to AdMob, please refer to Google’s Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.
10. Children's Privacy➕

Taskprayuj is intended for users 18+. We do not knowingly collect data from individuals under 18. If discovered, such data will be deleted immediately.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy➕

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Significant changes will be notified within the app or on our website. Continued use of the app constitutes acknowledgment of updates.
